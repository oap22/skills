# Dead Ends

What has been ruled out, so it never gets paid for twice. **Check this before proposing any experiment.**

One line each, grep-optimized:

`- **<what was tried>** — <why it failed>. \`<run-id>\`. <what would make it worth revisiting>.`

That last clause matters — it's what separates a dead end from a permanent prohibition. A dead end at 8GB may not be one at 80GB.
